Privacy Policy for Kaydiye ERP

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Rajo Technology ("we", "us", or "our") operates Kaydiye ERP. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the Kaydiye ERP application.

1. Information We Collect

Kaydiye ERP may collect information that is necessary to provide inventory, sales, customer, supplier, accounting, and business management features.

This information may include:

  • Account information such as name, email address, and login information.
  • Business and company information.
  • Customer information such as names, phone numbers, email addresses, and addresses.
  • Supplier information.
  • Product and inventory information.
  • Sales, invoices, payments, and transaction information.
  • Images uploaded by authorized users, such as product or business images.
  • Technical information necessary for the operation and security of the application.

2. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Provide and operate Kaydiye ERP.
  • Manage inventory, products, customers, suppliers, and sales.
  • Generate invoices, receipts, and business reports.
  • Provide accounting and payment-related business management features.
  • Authenticate users and protect accounts.
  • Maintain and improve the application.
  • Detect and prevent unauthorized access, fraud, or misuse.
  • Provide customer support.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information to third parties. Information may be shared with service providers only when necessary to operate, maintain, secure, or provide features of the application.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and services.

4. Image Uploads

Kaydiye ERP may allow authorized users to upload images, including product or business-related images. Uploaded images are used to provide the application's business management features.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the application's services, maintain business records, comply with applicable legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

7. Children's Privacy

Kaydiye ERP is a business management application and is not directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for purposes unrelated to the operation of the service.

8. Third-Party Services

Kaydiye ERP may use third-party service providers to support application functionality, hosting, storage, authentication, analytics, or other technical services. These providers may process information only as necessary to provide their services.

9. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including requesting access, correction, deletion, or information about how your data is processed.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, we will update the "Last updated" date on this page.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Kaydiye ERP handles information, please contact Rajo Technology.
